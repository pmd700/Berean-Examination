### Words Must Be clickable

By “clickable,” I meant each Hebrew word should become its own **UI object**, not just part of one giant verse string.

Example:

```
מַשָּׂ֥א דִּבְרֵ֗י אָג֥וּר
```

Instead of rendering that as one flat text blob, the app would internally treat it like:

```
word 1: מַשָּׂ֥א
word 2: דִּבְרֵ֗י
word 3: אָג֥וּר
```

Then later, when you tap/click a Hebrew word, the app can show:

```
Word: דִּבְרֵ֗י
Lemma: not available yet
Gloss: not available yet
Morphology: not available yet
Strong’s: not available yet
```

So “clickable” is mainly for future interlinear features: word info, glosses, morphology, Strong’s numbers, highlighting, notes, etc.
